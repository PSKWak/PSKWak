### About Me

**Philomath** — someone who genuinely likes learning for its own sake, not just for the credential at the end of it. That's the throughline behind everything below: I build applied ML/AI systems that hold up in the real world — RAG pipelines, multi-agent systems, evaluation harnesses that separate what a model can do from what a human needs to check. I'm currently teaching myself mechanistic interpretability and AI alignment from the ground up, one reproduced paper and one TransformerLens notebook at a time.

That same curiosity reaches outside AI. I read widely — philosophy, economics, fiction — and I've represented policy work as a delegate at the World Bank Group Youth Summit and GLF Nairobi, turning case studies into real recommendations. I don't claim expertise I haven't earned; I just follow the question until it's resolved.

- 🌍 Former **IMF Extern** — Washington, DC
- 🏛️ **Delegate**: World Bank Group Youth Summit ('25, '26) · GLF Nairobi ('22) · upcoming Intl. Youth Conf. on AI
- 🤖 Learning **Mechanistic Interpretability**, building toward real alignment research
- 📊 Independent research on **AI & Labor Market Displacement**
- ⚔️ National **Fencing Gold Medalist**
